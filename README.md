# Delhivery_Case_Study
Delhivery Case Study - Optimizing Logistics & Data Processing
🔍 Overview
Delhivery, India’s largest fully integrated logistics provider, strives to develop an advanced operating system for commerce through state-of-the-art infrastructure, technology, and data-driven operations. This study focuses on logistics efficiency, trip duration trends, and optimal routing offering actionable recommendations to enhance Delhivery's operations.

🚀 Business Problem
Delhivery seeks to improve its data processing and forecasting capabilities by:

- Cleaning, sanitizing, and transforming raw logistics data
- Identifying critical demand variables affecting efficiency
- Providing forecasting insights for operational enhancements

📊 Key Business Insights
1️⃣ Trip & Transport Patterns:
- 14.5K trips recorded between September 22, 2018, and October 8, 2018.
- Two transport types utilized—Full Truck Load (FTL) & Carting:
  - FTL for intercity deliveries
  - Cart for intra-city logistics
- Most trips commence early in the morning to optimize traffic conditions.

2️⃣ Warehouse & Regional Logistics Performance:
- Major sourcing hubs Bengaluru, Gurgaon—indicating the largest warehouses.
- High delivery concentration in Bengaluru, Gurgaon, and Mumbai, reflecting regional demand spikes.
- Frequent cross-state deliveries in Haryana, Punjab, and Delhi, signifying crucial interstate logistics corridors.

3️⃣ Route Efficiency & Traffic Impact:
- Fastest Routes:
  - Abohar - Malout (69.83/hr)
  - Shahdol - Anuppur (68.78 km/hr)
  - Jaisalmer - Pokhran (59.89 km/hr)
- Slowest Routes (High Congestion & Poor Road Conditions):
  - Mariani - Jorhat (2.16 km/hr)
  - Malvan - Sawantwadi (2.14 km/hr)
  - Khatra - Hura (1.88 km/hr)
- Top 5 Cities Where Trucks Arrived Quickly: Gomoh, Kanth, Kawardha, Tezu, Ghatampur.

📈 Strategic Recommendations
1️⃣ Expand Warehouse Infrastructure for Logistics Optimization:
- Increase warehouse hubs in high-demand cities (Bengaluru, Gurgaon, Mumbai) to red.
